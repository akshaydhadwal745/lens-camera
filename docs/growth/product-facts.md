# Lens product facts (for content and the growth analyst)

The only product claims content may make. The growth analyst names features from the **Features**
list exactly as written (the report is checked against these names); anything under **Not
available** must never be promised. Update this file when the product changes
(docs/README.md status table is the source).

Last checked: 2026-10-30.

## Features
- **Full-quality backup**: every photo and video goes to the cloud the moment it's taken, byte for byte; no compression, no "storage saver".
- **Free storage**: signed-in accounts get 40 GB free for the first 3 months, then 10 GB free; guests (no sign-in) get 5 GB.
- **Lens Plus**: 50 GB for ₹59 a month or ₹499 a year (website and website APK; Google Play billing is coming).
- **Extra storage**: above Plus, add storage at ₹3 per GB a month (website).
- **Phone storage saver**: originals are safe in the cloud, so the phone can free space while the gallery keeps working.
- **Pro camera**: manual controls matched to what the phone's camera hardware supports, from budget phones to flagships.
- **Night mode**: hold still for a moment for a clean, detailed low-light photo.
- **Portrait mode**: background blur that stays editable (stronger, softer or off later); the person outline is made on the phone.
- **Looks and editor**: pick a look before shooting and see it live, or edit later; edits are saved as a recipe and can be changed or removed.
- **Use your own storage**: save originals to Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, S3-compatible storage or a NAS (WebDAV).
- **Move to Google Drive**: move what's in Lens storage to the person's own Google Drive; the first 10 GB are free, then ₹15 per GB.
- **Download everything**: a free export of the whole library.
- **Share links**: share photos and videos in original quality with a link; the other person needs no app.
- **Event albums**: a QR code or link guests open in their browser to add and see photos (weddings, parties, trips); no app needed for guests.
- **4K video streaming**: long 4K videos start right away on another phone or the web at the best quality the connection allows.
- **Web app**: see, download and share the library from any browser.
- **People**: photos grouped by face on the phone (not in the EU/EEA, UK, Switzerland or the US).
- **Referrals**: +10 GB for each friend who backs up 20 photos (up to +100 GB); the friend gets an extra welcome month.
- **Trash and archive**: deleted items stay in Trash 30 days, then Archive 1 year, both recoverable.
- **Languages**: app and website in English and Hindi.
- **Android app**: on Google Play (closed test now) and as an APK from the website.

## Not available (never promise)
- An iPhone app for the public (iOS is built but not released).
- Unlimited storage.
- A desktop app (the web app works on computers).
- AI search, AI edits or automatic albums beyond People and event albums.
- Lens Plus bought inside the Google Play app (coming).
- Video calls, chat or social feeds.

## Positioning (how to describe Lens)
- Lead with the problem: "Phone full? Shoot everything." and full quality, not with gigabytes.
- Honest comparisons: Google Photos is a good product; Lens differs on original quality on the free tier, own-storage options, guest event albums without an app, and a pro camera.
